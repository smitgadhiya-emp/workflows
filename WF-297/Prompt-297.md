Two things will make a naive count wrong, so handle them explicitly. A subscription call site is not a subscription instance. Channels are not connections, and this is where the whole model lives or dies : Do not assume one subscription equals one WebSocket. Supabase multiplexes multiple channels over a single WebSocket per client instance, so eight `supabase.channel()` calls sharing one initialized client are eight channels riding one socket, while eight separate client instantiations are eight actual sockets. Those two situations produce answers that differ by 8x, and only one of them is true for our code. So go look at how the client is created in this repo: whether there's a single shared singleton client that everything imports, or whether clients get created per component, per hook, or per route. Report the channel count and the socket count as two separate numbers and never collapse them into one.
