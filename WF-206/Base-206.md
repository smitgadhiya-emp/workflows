Scans all Express.js route handlers to identify every point where req.body, req.params, req.query, and req.headers are accessed. For each access point, checks whether validation middleware (express-validator, Joi, Zod, celebrate) is applied before the handler executes and whether it covers the exact fields being accessed. Detects the coverage gap pattern where validation middleware exists on a route but only validates 3 of 7 fields the handler actually uses. Flags routes where multipart upload handling (multer) processes file inputs before the file's MIME type, size, and filename are validated - the specific pattern behind the Express.js ReDoS CVE published May 2026. Calculates what percentage of total user-reachable input surface area has zero validation and ranks unvalidated inputs by exploitability score. Exports the validation coverage map to Google Sheets with route, field, validation status, and OWASP risk category per gap.
